## Next, in order

1. **Fair comparisons.** Results grouped by company, Gemini (which answers without web search) shown apart, and competitors held to the same name rules as the business.
2. **A report an owner can act on.** A two-page owner summary with three actions, each tied to the answers behind it, and a separate brief for the web developer.
3. **Sound technical advice.** Search crawlers and training crawlers as separate choices, llms.txt optional, and each action marked as observed or to be checked.
4. **Site, checkout and measurement.** A shorter home page, a details step before payment, and measurement that follows a visit from the free check to a paid report without collecting personal details.
5. **A small, measured launch.** Five business owners try the check and the sample without help. Then a launch to one type of business through at most two channels, measured over the first 100 completed free checks.
6. **"Who does AI pick?" studies.** Which businesses AI models recommend, by trade and city, published with their method.
