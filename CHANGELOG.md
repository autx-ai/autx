# Changelog

What changed for customers, by date.

## November 11, 2026

- The How it works page became shorter. It explains what we ask, which models we ask and what the grade means. We no longer publish the detailed counting rules or the full question lists. The method itself did not change.
- We replaced the documents in this repository, this changelog included, with shorter versions.
- The questions and answers page gained six answers and now holds 56 questions. The new answers cover how the check differs from an AI visibility tracker, why the grade comes only with the full report, why scores from different tools cannot be compared, and what AUTX does not offer.
- The terms of service now forbid copying our questions, our scoring or the design of the report to offer a competing service. The data of our published studies stays free to reuse under its license.

## November 9, 2026

- The site got a new design, built around a report card.
- AUTX ran the full report on itself and published the result at [autx.ai/our-grade](https://autx.ai/our-grade/): F, 0 out of 100. AI models named AUTX in 0 of 112 answers to the questions customers ask about AI visibility tools. AUTX will run the report again every month and post the new grade, whatever it is.

## November 3, 2026

- A [questions and answers page](https://autx.ai/faq/) went live, with 50 questions in 9 topics.

## November 2, 2026

- AUTX published a study, [Who does AI pick?](https://autx.ai/studies/who-does-ai-pick/): eight AI models, 30 questions about local businesses in five US cities, asked twice on November 1, 2026. The data is free to use under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license (CC BY 4.0).
- A launch offer began. Through November 30, 2026, the full report costs $39.20 instead of $49, with code LAUNCH20 applied at checkout.

## November 1, 2026

- The full report went from six AI models to eight. Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro joined, both through Microsoft Foundry with web search on.
- Claude Sonnet 5.5 replaced Claude Sonnet 5. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite replaced Gemini 3.8 Flash and still answered without web search.
- GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna moved to Microsoft Foundry, where Grok 4.7, Muse Spark 1.3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro already ran.
- A new refund rule began: if a full report is still incomplete 24 hours after payment, AUTX refunds the order in full.
- A new eight-model run on the same Austin coffee shop, with its identity hidden, replaced the sample report.
- The full report gained a summary for the owner at the front and a checklist of every task.
- Report emails began to attach a developer brief, a text file for whoever looks after the website or listings.
- The buyer's private order page began to show a summary of the report and to offer the developer brief as a download.
- The site's own script began to count page views and clicks as daily totals, with no cookie and no ID. The [privacy policy](https://autx.ai/privacy/) describes these totals and names Cloudflare Web Analytics, which was already running.
- How it works, the free check and the rest of the site began to call the third result "Cited your website", as the report does, in place of "Linked".

## October 31, 2026

- A [privacy policy](https://autx.ai/privacy/) and [terms of service](https://autx.ai/terms/) went live. AUTX deletes report data 12 months after payment.
- The full report gained an AI visibility grade, a letter from A to F with a score out of 100. The score came from how often the answers named the business, recommended it and linked to its website. An incomplete report got no grade, and the free check had none.
- Owners could list up to three strengths on the free check form, such as outdoor seating. The free check and the full report asked one question about each strength, without the business's name. Strength answers got their own section and stayed out of the grade and the headline figures.
- Fix: the free check had skipped its name check from launch on October 28 until October 31.
- A six-model run with two strengths replaced the sample report.

## October 30, 2026

- The full report went from three AI models to six. Grok 4.7, Gemini 3.8 Flash (without web search) and Perplexity Sonar joined Claude, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna.
- A six-model run on an Austin coffee shop, with its identity hidden, replaced the sample report.

## October 29, 2026

- The product became the AUTX AI Visibility Check (free) and the AUTX AI Visibility Report ($49).
- The home page became the check, with a question set for shops, cafés, restaurants, salons, gyms and studios. The site added a sample report and a How it works page.
- Pages for business owners began to name AI companies instead of model codes. The site added a guide to getting recommended by AI assistants and an About page.
- The x402 services for developers moved to [autx.ai/x402](https://autx.ai/x402/).

## October 28, 2026

- Live Stripe payments opened for the $49 report.

## October 20, 2026

## October 17 to 19, 2026
