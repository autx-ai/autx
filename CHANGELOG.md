# Changelog

## 1 November 2026

- The full report asks eight AI models: Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro join, both through Microsoft Foundry with web search on. Muse Spark 1.3 is in preview on Azure.
- The full report uses each AI company's current model. Claude Sonnet 5.5 replaces Claude Sonnet 5. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, the newest version of the Flash-Lite model Google gives free Gemini app users, replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash and still answers without web search.
- GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna move to Microsoft Foundry, where Grok 4.7, Muse Spark 1.3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro already run.
- The sample report shows a new eight-model run on the same Austin coffee shop, with its identity hidden.
- The full report opens with a two-page summary for the owner. Page 1 gives the grade, a one-sentence conclusion, the three measures and the first three things to do. Page 2 gives one row per AI company, the competitors the owner listed and the strength answers. Page 3 holds a checklist of every task. Then come each task's steps and the numbered evidence.
- Report emails now attach a developer brief, a text file for whoever looks after the website or listings. It lists every task under the same task numbers as the PDF, with full steps for each website task.
- The buyer's private order page shows a summary of the report and offers the developer brief as a download.
- The site's own script counts page views and clicks as daily totals, with no cookie and no ID. The [privacy policy](https://autx.ai/privacy/) describes these totals and now also names Cloudflare Web Analytics, which was already running.
- How it works, the free check and the rest of the site now call the third result "Cited your website", as the report does, in place of "Linked".
- Fix: an answer that writes the business's web address with www or another subdomain, such as www.example.com, now counts as citing its website. Before, only the bare domain counted in the answer text. Subdomains already counted in cited sources.

## 31 October 2026

- A [privacy policy](https://autx.ai/privacy/) and [terms of service](https://autx.ai/terms/) went live. AUTX deletes report data 12 months after payment.
- The full report opens with an AI visibility grade, a letter from A to F with a score out of 100. The score comes from how often the answers name the business, recommend it and link to its website. A report gets no grade when too many answers are missing, and the free check has no grade.
- Owners can list up to three strengths on the free check form, such as outdoor seating. The free check and the full report ask one question about each strength, without the business's name. Strength answers get their own section and stay out of the grade and the headline figures.
- When some answers say the name is shared but more answers describe the business, the name check now reads "mixed", not "collision". Reports give both counts. The phrase "several different" now signals a shared name only before a word such as "businesses" or "things".
- Fix: the free check skipped its name check from launch on 28 October until 31 October. Its spending cap was too low, and AUTX raised it.
- The sample report shows a new six-model run with two strengths.
- The home page adds a section titled "Google rankings aren't the whole story anymore".
